# BEDROCK

Spydr-inspired BEDROCK starter.

## Flow
- `/` rewrites to `/assets/static/cloak.html`.
- The intro video at `/assets/images/load.mp4` plays first when provided.
- When the intro finishes, the BEDROCK welcome popup appears.
- Clicking **aw...yeah!!** navigates to `/index.html`.

## Add your assets
- Put the intro video at `assets/images/load.mp4`.
- Put your logo at `assets/images/logo.png` when you make it.

The homepage menu links are wired, but destination pages are placeholders for future work.
